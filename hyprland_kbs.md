### Hyprland KBs

| Super Mod ||
-|-
Arrows | Change window
Shift + Arrows | Resize window in that direction
Numbers | Change workspace
Shift + Numbers | Move active window to workspace
C | Kill window (Alt-F4 also works, usually)
Q | Terminal
E | File Manager
R | Launcher
J | Change split direction
Space | Toggle floating
Left Click | Drag window
V | Clipboard history

| Print Mod ||
-|-
Ctrl | Screenshot region
Alt | Start/stop recording main monitor
