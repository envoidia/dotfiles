### Hyprland KBs

| Super Mod ||
-|-
Arrows | Change focused window
Alt-Arrows | Move focused window
Shift-Arrows | Resize focused window
Space | Toggle floating of focused window
{Num} | To {Num} workspace
Shift-{Num} | Move active window to {Num} workspace
J | Change split direction
LClick | Drag window
RClick | Resize window
C | Kill focused window
Q | Open Terminal
R | Open Launcher
V | Open Clipboard history
A | Open Browser
S | Open Editor
M | Close Hyprland

| Other ||
-|-
Print | Screenshot region
Shift-Print | Start/stop recording main monitor
Ctrl-Print | Start/stop recording region
